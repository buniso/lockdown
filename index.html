What actually makes a country happy?

A data narrative built on the World Happiness Report 2020: tracing where the Cantril ladder and lived experience line up, and where GDP alone stops telling the whole story.

  • Research Question 01 How well does logged GDP per capita predict a country's ladder score, and where does the prediction break down?
  • Research Question 02 Which non-economic factors — social support, freedom, generosity — explain the gap when GDP falls short?
  • Research Question 03 Why does Sub-Saharan Africa show some of the widest, and most debated, divergence between predicted and reported happiness?

The short version

When discussing people's satisfaction with their lives, we consider money, freedom, safety, employment status, and more — yet GDP is still the number most often used to stand in for all of it. This project compares each country's actual ladder score against a GDP-based prediction, then looks closely at the countries — especially across Sub-Saharan Africa — that don't fit the expectation.
